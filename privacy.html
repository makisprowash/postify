Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Postify ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered vehicle listing generator used by automotive dealership employees. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Postify:

Vehicle listing data: When you submit a vehicle URL or enter vehicle details manually, we process that publicly available vehicle information (year, make, model, price, mileage, photos) to generate Facebook Marketplace listing content.

Facebook account data: When you connect your Facebook account, we receive an access token that allows us to post listings to Facebook Marketplace on your behalf. We do not store your Facebook password.

Usage data: We may collect basic usage information such as the number of listings generated and features used, for the purpose of improving the service.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

• Generate AI-powered Facebook Marketplace listing content for vehicle inventory
• Post vehicle listings to Facebook Marketplace on your behalf when authorized
• Improve and maintain the Postify platform
• Communicate with you about the service

Facebook Data

Postify integrates with the Facebook Graph API to enable listing creation on Facebook Marketplace. When you connect your Facebook account, we request only the permissions necessary to create and manage vehicle listings. We do not sell or share your Facebook data with third parties. You may disconnect your Facebook account at any time from within the Postify application.

Data Storage and Security

Vehicle listing data is processed in real time and is not permanently stored on our servers beyond what is necessary to complete the listing generation. Facebook access tokens are encrypted at rest. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Third-Party Services

Postify uses the following third-party services:

• Anthropic Claude API — for AI content generation
• Facebook Graph API — for Marketplace listing publication
• Vercel — for application hosting

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data associated with your account by contacting us. You may also revoke Facebook permissions at any time through your Facebook account settings.
